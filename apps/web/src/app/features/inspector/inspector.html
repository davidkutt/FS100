Ranked hits for a query, as the chat retrieves them: dense and sparse top 50, fused, reranked. Each section appears once, through its best-matching chunk; the chat gets the whole section. Ranks: rerank = position, fused = rank among the reranker's candidates, dense / sparse = the chunk's rank on that side (– = not in its top 50); the score is the reranker's, without one the dense similarity. A priority boost moves a hit by positions when the question is on the document's priority sub-topics and the hit clears the chat's threshold.
